# The Vision

Imagine a world where every human being — whether in a high-rise in Frankfurt, a tin-roof home in Nairobi, or a refugee camp in Lebanon — can become an equal member of a global federation for just €1 per month. A federation not controlled by governments, corporations, or billionaires, but governed equally by all. OnePlanet is the digital constitution of a new, supranational democracy: a system where your voice carries mathematically equal weight to every other person on this planet, where manipulation is made impossible through zero-knowledge cryptography, where a guardian network of trusted people protects your digital identity the way neighbors once safeguarded your house key, and where every cent flows transparently on a public blockchain — into education, climate action, emergency relief, and open technology. OnePlanet is not another blockchain project and not another donation platform — it is the attempt to build the infrastructure for planetary self-governance: an operating system for humanity, running on equality, ethics, and radical transparency, from the first line of code to the last vote cast.

---

## Why This Matters

- **7.9 billion people** have no say in the decisions that shape their planet
- **Democratic erosion** is accelerating worldwide
- **Climate, migration, health** — the biggest challenges are supranational, but our governance is not
- **Technology exists** to make global, fair, transparent self-governance possible — it just hasn't been built yet

OnePlanet is the attempt to build it.

## Core Principles

1. **One person, one voice** — no plutocracy, no special rights for donors
2. **Privacy by default** — zero-knowledge proofs, not surveillance
3. **Radical transparency** — every transaction, every vote, every decision is public
4. **Ethics first** — hardcoded into the constitution, not an afterthought
5. **Accessible to all** — offline paths, multi-language, WCAG compliance
6. **Open source** — every line of code, every document, every process
